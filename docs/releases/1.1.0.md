# OpenOnion Messages 1.1.0

OpenOnion Messages no longer replaces the phone's texting app. It now runs
alongside it and forwards a copy of each new SMS, encrypted, to one paired
ConnectOnion Agent. Pairing is a QR scan instead of a pasted link.

## Changed

- **Your texting app stays the default.** 1.0.0 had to become the default SMS
  app, which took over sending, contacts, MMS, group chats and RCS without
  doing them well. Android already tells every app with SMS access about new
  texts, so the role was never needed for forwarding. The local inbox,
  composer and per-message delete are removed; read and delete in the Agent
  inbox.
- **One permission.** The app asks only for `RECEIVE_SMS` (plus the camera
  while scanning). `READ_SMS`, `SEND_SMS` and notifications are gone.
- **Scan to pair.** Run `co sms pair`, tap **Scan pairing code**, and point the
  camera at the terminal. Dark and light terminal themes both read. Pasting the
  link remains a fallback.
- **Pair first.** Connecting the Agent is the first step. The six-digit check
  is a full-screen page, and success leads straight to turning on forwarding.
- **Sideloaded installs.** When Android 13+ silently blocks SMS access for an
  APK installed outside an app store, the app shows how to allow restricted
  settings.

## Upgrading from 1.0.0

Install over 1.0.0; the signing key is unchanged and pairing is kept. The app
then asks you to switch back to your usual texting app: tap **Choose SMS app**
and pick Messages. Forwarding continues.

## Compatible services

- `oo-api` at `https://oo.openonion.ai` with the v2 signed pairing routes;
- ConnectOnion with `co sms pair`. Releases that print a QR work; pressing
  Enter to approve, instead of typing `y`, arrives with connectonion PR #2722.

## Known limitations

- Only SMS is forwarded. MMS and RCS chat messages reach your texting app as
  usual but are not sent to the Agent.
- Messages received before pairing are not uploaded.
- Android stops delivering SMS to an app that was force-stopped until it is
  opened again. Some Xiaomi, Huawei and OPPO builds also need auto-start
  allowed.
- Agents cannot send SMS or automatically act on SMS content.
